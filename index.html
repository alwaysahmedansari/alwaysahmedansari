About

Ideas, examined carefully.

Ahmed Ansari is an independent author and researcher based in Lahore, Pakistan. His work explores the intersections of history, politics and society, human psychology, religion and philosophy, and how intellectual traditions and social structures shape human behaviour. Writing in both English and Urdu, he focuses on analytical essays, cultural reflections and long-form research that encourage critical thinking, emotional awareness and purposeful reading.

Chapter One: Understanding Human Psychology and the Emotional Chess Game

An educational and analytical exploration of human psychology, emotional manipulation and relationship dynamics through a case-based narrative. This chapter examines the Saviour Complex, Trauma Bonding, Intermittent Reinforcement, Cognitive Dissonance, Mirroring, Passive-Aggression, Moving the Goalposts, Spiritual Bypassing, Narcissistic Injury, DARVO and Guilt-Tripping, and stresses emotional awareness, critical thinking and healthy boundaries over self-diagnosis. Originally written in Urdu and translated and edited into English with the assistance of AI.

ArticleNovember 26, 2026

From the House of Wisdom to Forgotten Libraries

An exploration of how a civilisation that once led the world in scholarship, scientific research and intellectual achievement gradually drifted away from books and the pursuit of knowledge. Drawing on Baghdad’s House of Wisdom and the era of Al-Ma’mun, the article reflects on our forgotten intellectual heritage and the urgent need to revive a culture of reading, questioning and research.
